STEP 02 / 24

Put these items on the table

  1. Your vending machine and batteries matching its battery markings.
  2. A Mac or Windows laptop with Chrome, Bluetooth, internet, and a wallet extension.

Everything is within reach.

Use the laptop for the full wallet + Bluetooth test. Android Chrome can test Bluetooth, but ordinary Android Chrome has no injected wallet extension; iPhone browsers do not support this direct Bluetooth flow.

STEP 03 / 24

Check the actual machine

  1. Find the model label on the back or base. Photograph it for your own records.
  2. Confirm four compartments: tall left; top right; middle right; bottom right.
  3. Mark these positions A, B, C, D with removable labels.
Done when

Four physical compartments are identified.

A–D are labels we are assigning now. They are not verified factory Bluetooth channel numbers. If your unit differs from the listing, stop and use its actual model label.

STEP 04 / 24

Check the battery specification

  1. Read the label on the battery pack or inside the battery holder.
  2. Note the battery type and quantity, or the pack specification.
  3. Match the holder’s + and − markings. If the label is missing, ask the seller before choosing batteries.
Done when

You know the correct battery specification and orientation.

Your unit runs only on batteries, with no external power supply. Battery type, quantity, and rechargeability still need confirming.

STEP 05 / 24

Prepare the battery-powered machine

  1. Place the cabinet flat and stable. Remove only obvious transport packaging.
  2. Fit batteries only as marked in a user-accessible holder. If already fitted, use any labeled power or wake control.
  3. Leave all stock out. Gently close the four compartments; do not force a stuck latch.
Done when

The specified batteries are fitted; all four compartments are empty.

The wake procedure and indicators are unverified. Next, check Bluetooth. Rechargeability is still unknown.

  3. Close the seller app and any other app currently connected to this machine.

The web app does not require the vendor account to test the supplied direct-Bluetooth protocol.

STEP 07 / 24

Connect to the nearby machine

  1. Click Connect machine. A Chrome Bluetooth chooser appears.
  2. Select your unit and click Pair. If absent, cancel and enable Show all nearby devices, then try once more.
  3. Wait for “Handshake confirmed.” Record the exact device name below.
Done when

The page says Handshake confirmed.

Do not pair a random nearby device. If the name is unclear, compare the chooser before and after turning your own machine off, then back on. Never hold undocumented reset buttons.

STEP 08 / 24

If the handshake fails, stop here

  1. Keep the unit empty. Open Activity and export the session log.
  2. Try the seller’s original APK on an Android device, if available. The supplied file is base.apk.1; copy it as KSJ-Bluetooth.apk if Android requires an .apk extension.
  3. Use the account the seller gave you privately. Compare whether that app can connect to this unit.
Done when

Either the handshake works, or you have a log and a clear failure to send the seller.

The APK is an Android tool; Chrome cannot install or run it. Do not put the seller password into our web app. If neither app connects, the seller must confirm the correct firmware/protocol. No purchase test yet.

STEP 09 / 24

Check the closed-door readings

  1. With all physical compartments closed, click Refresh status.
  2. Confirm the displayed channels report CLOSED.
  3. If the screen says OPEN while the doors are closed, disconnect, open Machine settings, and check the sensor-polarity setting with the next physical test.
Done when

The readings match the physical closed state.

The controller may report 12 channel values even though this cabinet has four compartments. That is protocol capacity, not extra physical doors.

STEP 10 / 24

Test channel 1 once

  1. Tick “I’m beside my machine” to arm controls.
  2. Click Test door 1, then Open door 1 once.
  3. Watch which physical compartment opens. Write A, B, C, or D next to channel 1 below.
Done when

One physical compartment opened, and its sensor reports OPEN.

The operator screen’s numbers are controller channels. Do not assume channel 1 means the tall left compartment. A timeout means unknown: inspect the door before doing anything else.

STEP 11 / 24

Close it and test channels 2–4

  1. Close the compartment by hand. Click Refresh status and confirm CLOSED.
  2. Repeat the same single-open test for channels 2, 3, and 4, closing each before the next.
  3. Record which channel opens each physical position.
Done when

All four positions have distinct, physically verified channel mappings.

If any channel does nothing or opens the wrong position, do not guess channels 5–12. Save the log and get the seller’s wiring/channel map.

STEP 12 / 24

Check reconnecting before stocking

  1. Disconnect in the operator app, leaving the batteries in place. If a power or wake control is present, follow its label or the seller’s instructions.
  2. Reconnect and wait for Handshake confirmed.
  3. Refresh status. Check all four physical compartments and readings again.
Done when

A fresh connection gives the same four verified sensor readings.

This separates a repeatable setup from a one-time lucky connection.

STEP 16 / 24

Enter the machine’s verified settings

  1. Enter the exact Bluetooth name recorded earlier.
  2. Fill the channel for A, B, C, and D using your physical test notes.

STEP 17 / 24

Stock one compartment

  1. Disconnect the customer page if connected. Use Operator controls to open the chosen empty compartment.
  2. Place one test item inside. Close the compartment and verify CLOSED. Then disconnect Operator controls.
  3. Back in Machine setup, expand Stock a compartment on-chain. Choose its position, product name, and TEST price.
Done when

One real item is behind one closed latch.

Treat a compartment as one sale, even if several small items fit inside. Unlocking gives access to the entire compartment.

STEP 18 / 24

Make that item available

  1. Tick “I placed one item inside and closed the compartment.”
  2. Click Make this item available and confirm the wallet transaction.
  3. Tick that you physically checked all four mappings, then click Save machine and open shop.
Done when

The shop shows the stocked product with its TEST price.

Only the wallet that created the contract can restock it. The contract marks the compartment sold out after one purchase.

STEP 19 / 24

Give the customer wallet test credits

  1. Open Machine setup again.
  2. Expand Create a fresh Sepolia test deployment and click “3. Get test credits.”
  3. Confirm the transaction from the wallet you will use to buy.
Done when

The buyer wallet has 100 free TEST credits.

You can use the same wallet for tomorrow’s supervised test. A separate buyer wallet also needs Sepolia ETH for gas.

STEP 20 / 24

Choose, connect, and pay

  1. Choose the stocked item. Click Connect this machine and select the same Bluetooth device.
  2. Tick that you are beside the machine. Click Pay.
  3. Approve the exact token amount in the wallet, then confirm the separate purchase transaction.
Done when

The app says Payment confirmed, after two confirmations.

Approval alone is not a purchase. The compartment does not open just because a transaction was submitted. Keep the browser open.

STEP 21 / 24

Collect the item

  1. Click Open my compartment once.
  2. Confirm the correct physical compartment opens. Take the item and close the door.
  3. Click I collected my item.
Done when

The correct compartment opened, the item was collected, and the shop marks it sold out.

If pickup is uncertain, do not pay again. Save the receipt and ask the operator to inspect the compartment. Payment and physical delivery are different events.

STEP 22 / 24

If payment succeeds but pickup fails

  1. Save the receipt from the customer screen. Note the order ID.
  2. Inspect the physical compartment before retrying anything.
  3. If no item was supplied, the operator can use Machine setup → Refund a failed pickup and enter the order ID.
Done when

The outcome is resolved without a second charge.

A refund does not reopen or restock a compartment. Restock only after inspecting the machine and placing an item inside.
